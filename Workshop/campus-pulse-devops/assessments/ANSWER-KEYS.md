# Assessment Answer Keys

## Pre-assessment

1 A—`pwd` prints the working directory. 2 B—a pipe connects stdout to the next stdin. 3 C—the execute bit permits execution. 4 B—a process is a running program. 5 C—`commit` records staged changes. 6 B—CI integrates and verifies frequently. 7 B—an image is the packaged template used to create containers. 8 A—2xx is successful. 9 C—use a scoped secret store and never logs/source. 10 B—a health check provides an operational readiness signal.

## Post-assessment

1 A—`ss -ltn` lists listening TCP sockets. 2 B—`>` truncates/overwrites; `>>` appends. 3 D—600 grants only owner read/write. 4 A—already-running processes do not automatically receive later shell changes. 5 B—observe ownership before changing anything. 6 A—branches isolate change for review. 7 B—tests should be repeatable and behavior-focused. 8 A—stable dependency layers remain cached. 9 B—least privilege limits damage. 10 B—Actions only discovers pushed files at the required path with a matching event. 11 A—shell exit codes communicate success/failure. 12 B—health is an automated release gate. 13 B—rollback restores known-good behavior. 14 A—each telemetry type answers a different question. 15 B—rotation stops further use; repository cleanup alone does not.

Suggested interpretation: 13–15 secure, 10–12 competent, 7–9 developing, 0–6 needs guided review. Compare individual pre/post totals; do not claim causal learning from a single small cohort.
